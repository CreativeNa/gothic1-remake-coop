# Status

Last updated: 2026-10-31

> **Important note on testing.** Most of the changes described here were **not tested in real play situations**. Everything was developed and checked only with two copies of the game on **one PC**, mostly through automated scripts and synthetic actions (teleports, destroying an actor instead of a real pickup, direct state writes), not by people playing together. Real two-PC / internet play, long sessions and many ordinary game situations (dialogues, trading, normal looting, longer fights, save / load cycles) have not been exercised. Treat every feature as an experimental first version that **needs further work and real-world testing**.

> **Важно о тестировании.** Большая часть изменений **не проверялась в реальных игровых ситуациях**. Всё разрабатывалось и проверялось только на двух копиях игры на **одном компьютере**, в основном автоматическими скриптами и искусственными действиями (телепорты, удаление объекта вместо настоящего подбора, прямая запись состояний), а не живой игрой вдвоём. Игра по сети между двумя ПК, долгие сессии и обычные игровые ситуации не проверялись. Каждая функция — экспериментальная первая версия, которая **требует доработки и проверки в реальных условиях**.

## Done

- Native DLL injection, engine access by fixed RVAs, game-thread hook, ProcessEvent calls.
- Puppet of the other player (hero mesh), position and rotation sync over UDP (localhost).
- Full-skeleton pose sync (422 bones, chunked UDP datagrams), forced LOD0, face / armor / IK helper bones left in reference pose.
- Equipment mirrored on the puppet (quiver with arrow count, bow, sword, food in hand).
- Hot-reloadable modules (`anim`, `equip`, `pose`) with a small API.
- Connecting by IP: host names or addresses, packets accepted only from the configured friend (`tools/join.ps1`). Tested on one PC only.
- CouchLink recipe for two copies on one PC (`tools/couchlink/`); the two players see each other and animations keep working.

## Known problems

- **Skin stretching / "spike"** on the puppet, mainly seen with the sword drawn. Several causes were found and fixed (bones returning identity at the origin, only part of the bones sent, parent-chain skipping, NaN guard). Not proven fully gone; re-check with the sword drawn from both windows after every pose change.
- `SetBoneTransformByName` works only in component space; children are converted through the parent's cached pose, so fast motion can leave small errors.
- No facial animation; dark patches on the puppet's face in some lighting.
- FPS dips during fights (one engine call per bone).
- **Crash `0xd50`: root cause found, fix in place, needs a long soak test.** The game's own `BFGTickOptimizerSystem` (with `BFGInterpolationSystem`) tracks the puppet actor (`IsActorRegisteredAndActive` = 1) and faults inside its tick (`exe+0x363dfff`, `cmp [rcx+0xd50],0` with a null tracked entry) when the player runs away from it ("hit, then run away"). Bisecting showed: no puppet = no crash; puppet actor alone (no modules) = no crash; a bare `PoseableMeshComponent` or one with only the mesh set = no crash; attached/bone-driven = crash. The pose module now opts the puppet out of the optimizer with `SetTickOptimizationRuntime_Enabled(Actor, false)` (re-checked every 3 s). Details of the old investigation:
- Crash (access violation reading `0xd50`) a few seconds after swinging the sword near the puppet. The AV recorder pins it inside the game itself: `G1R-Win64-Shipping.exe+0x363dfff` is `cmp qword ptr [rcx+0xd50], 0` at the start of a method called with `this == NULL`; no coopdll frames on the stack. Prime suspect: the local sword hits the other player's owner-less item visual or the puppet mesh. Mitigation tried: collision fully disabled on item actors and on the poseable mesh (not yet verified). Previous mitigations: no destroy/respawn of the sword visual (re-attach instead), clip layer paused while the pose module drives the puppet.
- Equipment attachments to the puppet were once floating next to the body; re-check.
- **Arrows (parked, 2026-10-30):** the bow now shows on the puppet (it needs the hero as spawn owner). The nocked arrow in the hand sits wrongly (the hero's `arrow` socket hangs on an undriven IK bone; mapped to `weapon_r` it points down). The friend's shot is detected and an arrow visual is flown by `equip_module.c` (`shot_tick`, test trigger: create `native\build\shoot_test.txt`), positions are applied, but the arrow was not seen on screen (class is a real `ArrowVisual` projectile; maybe hidden until the game starts it). Next: check visibility, drop or fix the nocked arrow, send aim pitch.
- Arrow count on the puppet's quiver can only go down.
- **Resolved (2026-10-30):** equipment / quick-slot bar missing in the second copy was caused by GPU memory pressure (two copies on an 8 GB card); with memory freed everything shows. If it returns, lower texture quality for both copies in the Couchlink recipe.

## Shared world (2026-10-31)

- **Per-NPC ownership** (`npc_module.c`): the copy whose hero is clearly nearer to an NPC (600 units nearer) simulates it (AI, hits, health, knock-out); the other copy mirrors it. Everything starts with seat 0. Both copies run the same code: each sends what it simulates (position, yaw, speed, bones of defeated NPCs) on the second blob channel and mirrors what the other one simulates. So **both players can fight NPCs**: the nearer one hits, the other sees the result.
- Mirrored NPCs have their collision off, and are placed every frame; defeated ones are bone-driven (a `PoseableMeshComponent` with the NPC's mesh asset, original mesh hidden with tick / physics / collision off, items re-attached, exact one-pass bone application, close-up-only helper bones left in the reference pose). Bone-driving non-defeated NPCs and `PlayAnimation` clips were dropped (stretched skin, a crash in the game's animation component).
- Verified by `tools\tests\run_all.ps1` (18 checks, about 8 minutes, no human): boot, NPC positions host vs guest by numbers, the host knocks Ratford out, the guest takes the NPC over when its hero is nearer and knocks it out, the host mirrors it bone-driven at the same place, two quick loads with an NPC down, fps, no exception in our code.
- Health numbers (and death) of an NPC and the weapon in its hand (melee / bow, holster <-> hand) follow the owner on the mirror (`health:` checks of the suite).
- Not done: loot / inventory of a dead NPC is left to each world's own death logic, NPCs that exist in only one copy because of streaming when the players are far apart (the owner has them loaded: ownership by proximity covers this), arrows in flight, two real PCs over the internet.

## Story and quests (2026-10-31)
- `native/story_module.c` (core 51, third channel `S|`): the whole story object (`StoryG1R`, ~520 int / time words incl. the chapter) and the `State` of all 679 quests are kept equal in both copies.
  The host sends everything on first contact (the guest takes over), after that only changes, repeated until the other copy acknowledges; a conflict is won by the host.
  Live quest changes go through the game's own `StartQuest` / `SucceedQuest` / `MakeQuestAvailable` (journal, rewards of the local hero), the first bulk sync and other states are written directly.
  Quest states: 0 none, 1 available, 2 running, 3 failed, 4 succeeded.
- Test: `tools\tests\test_story.ps1` (6 checks, both directions). Not yet verified visually: journal entries / notifications on the guest, dialogues in progress, rewards given twice.

## World items (2026-10-31)
- The story module also syncs pickups of items lying in the world: an item that gets destroyed within 450-2500 units of its hero (one at a time, not right after a teleport) is destroyed in the other copy too; if the other copy has not loaded it yet it is removed when it appears.
  Items are matched by their persistent id, or (for items the level spawns at runtime, which have none) by class + position rounded to 25 units.
- The core's `obj_alive(ptr, name)` mis-reports objects that differ only by the number suffix of their name (the cache is per FName index); the module checks identity on the raw FName instead. Other modules still use the old call.
- Test: `tools\tests\test_items.ps1` (8 checks, both directions).
- NOT done: chests and bodies (contents are in the actor's DataModuleComponent, not examined), items dropped by a player, trading, each hero keeps its own inventory. Not tried: a real pickup by interaction (the test destroys the actor, which is what a pickup should do; not verified).

### Open problems reported by the user (2026-11-01)
- **Chapter start banner:** the banner that appears at the start of a story chapter is not synchronized: the second player does not get it (the chapter number itself is part of the synced story data, but the banner / HUD notification is a local event of the game that is not replayed).
- **Falling through the ground after the UluMulu cutscene:** after the `UluMuluPresentation` cutscene finished, the players fell through the textures and kept falling. Not investigated yet. Suspects: the cutscene teleports the hero to its cinematic waypoint and back while the world data of that place is not loaded (the test runs half-resolution copies, two of them, with world streaming under memory pressure), a cutscene started by a probe call (not by the story) so the "after" logic did not run, or our own placement of NPCs / the puppet; the test harness `Restore-Heroes` also teleports heroes right after a cutscene.
- **Diego's start conversation is not synchronized (reported 2026-11-01, live test with two copies):** on the first copy Diego stopped next to the player and asked something (the start dialogue); on the second copy he walked past and asked nothing. The NPC movement/ownership is synced, but the dialogue state is not, so the two copies diverge from the very first scene. Dialogues are not synchronized at all yet. Not investigated; needs a dialogue-start observer (as for the cutscenes) and a decision on which copy owns the dialogue.
- **Killing a meat bug (reported 2026-11-01, live test with two copies):** on the second copy the player attacked a meat bug and it did not die, but its entry in the bestiary (reference book) appeared on the second copy. On the first copy the player killed a bug and it died on the second copy too; the bestiary entry appeared on the second copy at the same moment as the "kill" there. So the bestiary entry is unlocked by the replicated death of the mirror, not by the player's own kill, and a hit made on the second copy does not reach the NPC owned by the first copy (damage from the guest is not sent). Needs: send the guest's hits/damage to the owner of the NPC, and decide which copy unlocks the bestiary. Not investigated.
- **Item pickup is delayed (reported 2026-11-01, live test with two copies):** a picked-up world item disappears on the other copy with a delay of about one second. Suspects: the world item scan on the other copy is time-budgeted per frame and runs on a timer, and the S channel sends changes in batches; to be measured (log timestamps of the pickup on both copies). Not fixed.
- **Chest locking (idea from the live test, 2026-11-01):** while one player has a chest open, the other player must not be able to open it (otherwise both take items from the same chest at once). Not implemented. Needs: an "opened by the friend" state sent on the S channel when the chest UI opens/closes (the container observers already see the chest functions), and a block (or a short "in use" refusal) on the other copy until the friend closes it, with a timeout in case the friend disconnects.
- **No chest open/close animation on the other copy (reported 2026-11-01, live test):** when one player opens or closes a chest, the lid animation is not shown on the other copy (only the emptied content is synced). Needs: send the chest open/close event (together with the chest locking above) and replay the chest's open/close function or animation on the mirror. Not investigated.
- **Items put INTO a chest are not synchronized (reported 2026-11-01, live test):** taking items out of a chest works in both directions, but when a player puts their own items into a chest, they do not appear in that chest on the other copy (also not after walking away and coming back). The chest sync only knows "emptied / item taken" (list lengths zeroed, `Server_ConsumeItem` replay), not "item added". Needs: observe the add-item container function (as for consume), send the item class and count, and replay it on the other copy. Not implemented.
- **Second copy cannot hurt enemies owned by the first copy (reported 2026-11-01, live test, molerat):** on the second copy the molerat behaves like an invisible enemy, the player's hits do not land. On the first copy the molerat could be hit and killed; its death animation was synchronized to the other copy with some twitching (jitter of the bone-driven body). Same cause as the meat bug: the guest's damage is not sent to the owner of the NPC. Needs: hit/damage events from the mirror copy to the owner copy.
- **Second copy cannot loot a killed enemy (reported 2026-11-01, live test):** the loot inside a killed enemy cannot be searched from the second copy; from the first copy everything can be searched. Probably the mirror body has no usable inventory/interaction on the guest side. To be investigated (body loot sync exists for the case of the owner looting; the reverse direction was not verified in real play).
- **IDEA - shared experience for kills (to remind the user):** award experience to both players when either one kills an enemy (the experience of the kill is shared, not only for the one who dealt the last hit). Not implemented. REMIND THE USER about this idea.
- **Update to the molerat report (2026-11-01, live test):** after the player started moving on the second copy, the molerat appeared again there, could be hit, killed and looted. So the "invisible, cannot be hit, cannot be looted" state is temporary and probably depends on NPC ownership: the NPC is owned by the nearest copy, and the other copy only has a mirror that takes no damage. When ownership moved to the second copy (the player came closer / moved), everything worked. The fix should not wait for ownership to change: a hit on a mirror must be sent to the owner (or ownership must switch to the attacker at the first hit), and a mirror body must be lootable.
- **TODO (later): what happens when a player dies.** Not implemented and not tested: the dead player gets the normal game-over / load screen, the friend's copy knows nothing about it and the world states of the two copies can drift apart after the load. Options discussed: (1) shared death - both load the last common save; (2) revive next to the friend, reload only when both are dead; (3) spectator mode until the friend reaches a safe place. Recommended start: option 1. The design decision is the user's. To be done later.
- **IDEA: shared save loading (to try later).** When the host or the guest loads a save, the other player should be taken to the same save too, so that one player does not stay where they were while the other appears somewhere else on the map (or even close by) - that breaks immersion and lets the world states drift apart. To try: observe the load-save action on one copy, send it (with the save name/slot) over the S channel, and have the other copy load the same save, with the join gate/handshake re-run after the load. Related to the player death TODO above (shared death = shared load). Design details (who may trigger it, asking the friend for confirmation, the saves being different on the two PCs) are open.
- **Character bodies: the work is not finished (reported 2026-11-01):** the bodies of killed characters still show breaks/distortions (twisted, broken-looking limbs and jitter of the bone-driven body on the copy that does not own the NPC). Not fixed yet. Related: death animation twitching on the other copy (molerat), and the mirror body being unusable (cannot be hit or looted from the second copy).
- **The puppet of the second player hangs in the air until that player has loaded (reported 2026-11-01):** while the second player is still in the main menu or loading a save, the puppet (the figure that represents them in the other copy's world) stays in the air at the last/initial position instead of being hidden or put on the ground. Needs: hide the puppet (or keep it at a safe position) until the friend is really in the world, e.g. by a "friend is in the world" flag in the pose packets, and show it only after the first valid pose.
- **Chapter banner now works in the live test (2026-11-01):** the banner of the first chapter appears on the other copy, with a delay of about one second. The delay comes from the story module, which looks for changes once a second (same cause as the item pickup delay). Possible improvement: compare the chapter number every frame and send it at once. Not done.

Proposed: compare the chapter number (and the small, frequently changing flags) every frame and send the change at once, and send item pickups immediately from the observer instead of from the periodic scan.
